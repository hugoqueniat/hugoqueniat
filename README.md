### 🧬 About Me

My research interests lie at the intersection of sampling and probabilistic machine learning 🎲. My current work focuses on stochastic simulation, with a particular emphasis on rare-event simulation and subsequent large deviations 📉. 

I am also broadly interested in mean-field game theory and optimal control problems, which I explore through the EU-funded ERC Consortium **OCEAN** 🌊. As an avid motorsports fan, I am keen on analyzing these mathematical frameworks through a racing lens 🏎️🏁.

🤝 **Collaborations:** If you are interested in collaborating or discussing potential research directions, please feel free to reach out via email!
